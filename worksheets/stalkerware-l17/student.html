What is stalkerware?

8 min · alone · read, then Task 1
stalkerwarean app that secretly watches another person's phone consentpermission: the person knows about it and says yes
monitorto watch and record what someone does permissiona setting that lets an app use something, e.g. the location or the microphone
hiddendifficult to see or find accountyour login for a service, e.g. a Google Account or an Apple Account

Stalkerware is an app that one person secretly installs on another person's phone to watch them. The app sends data to the person who installed it: text messages, call history, photos, the phone's location, and sometimes the microphone and everything the owner types. The person reads this data on a website, so they never need to touch the phone again.

Most often, the person is a partner or an ex-partner. On an Android phone, they usually need the phone and the PIN for a few minutes. Stalkerware is not in the official app store, so they install it from a file, and they often switch off Google Play Protect first. On an iPhone, they sometimes need only the email and password of the owner's Apple Account, not the phone. Two-factor authentication makes this much harder.

The app tries to stay hidden. It often has no icon, or a plain name like “System Service” or “Sync”. The owner may never notice it.

Stalkerware is not the same as a parental-control app or a work-phone app. Those apps are visible, and the people who use the phone know about them. In many countries, installing monitoring software on another adult's phone without permission is a crime.
